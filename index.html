Data source: National Environment Agency (NEA) open data API via data.gov.sg. Real-time regional 1-hr PM2.5 readings are recorded at five monitoring stations across Singapore.

Exposure Formula: Inhaled Index (µg·h/m³) = for each trip: NEA hourly reading × breathing factor × minutes × (1 − mask filtration). Breathing factors: 1.0 (waiting/standing), 1.5 (walking), 2.5 (brisk walk/cycling), 3.5 (jogging). Mask filtration estimates (incl. fit leakage): cloth ≈50%, surgical ≈65%, KN95 ≈80%, N95 ≈90%. The 24-hr equivalent average is index ÷ 24, which is what the risk bands compare against.
